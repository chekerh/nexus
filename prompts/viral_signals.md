# Viral Signals Rubric

Use this scoring lens when selecting hooks.

## Positive Signals
- Pattern interrupt (unexpected statement/event)
- High stakes (money, status, risk, loss, damage)
- Conflict and resolution
- Strong opinion or contrarian claim
- Concrete specificity (numbers, names, locations, price)
- Emotional charge (shock, fear, excitement, envy)
- Transformation arc (before/after)
- Practical insight with clear payoff

## Negative Signals
- Low-energy narration
- Context too thin to understand clip
- Excessive filler words or repetition
- No clear payoff by the end of window

## Window Construction Rules
- Start as close as possible to the trigger moment while preserving context.
- Include enough lead-in for comprehension.
- End right after the payoff sentence/event.
- Avoid abrupt cut-offs mid-sentence.

## Platform Bias
Optimize for short-form retention:
- first 3 seconds must create curiosity
- middle must escalate or reveal
- ending should resolve or open a loop worth commenting on
