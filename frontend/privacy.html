Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect your email address, display name, and account credentials when you register. Video files you upload are stored locally on your machine or your configured storage.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your email is used for account-related communications (receipts, password resets, service notifications). We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

3. Local Processing

All video processing happens locally on your machine using local AI models. Your video content is never uploaded to external servers for processing.

4. Third-Party Services

When you choose to publish to social platforms, content is sent directly to those platforms via their APIs. Stripe handles payment processing — we never store your payment details.

5. Data Retention

Your account data is retained until you delete your account. You may request deletion at any time by contacting support.

6. Security

We use encryption for data in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (encrypted OAuth tokens). Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256.

7. Contact

For privacy inquiries: support@nexusugc.com.
